About the Project

This AI-driven crop disease prediction and management system represents a breakthrough in precision agriculture. By leveraging advanced computer vision, machine learning, and deep learning technologies, we provide farmers and agricultural professionals with powerful tools to detect, identify, and manage crop diseases before they cause significant damage.

Our system analyzes leaf texture, color pigmentation, and surface abnormalities to provide accurate disease diagnosis with treatment recommendations, helping to improve crop yields and reduce economic losses.
